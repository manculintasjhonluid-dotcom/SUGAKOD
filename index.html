ABOUT THE PROJECT

ALAYON SA UMA

ALAYON SA UMA is an initiative focused on helping small farmers strengthen their access to agricultural market information and potential buyers.

SUGAKOD serves as an information component of the initiative. It provides a directory of potential buyers, geographic references, agricultural price information, and basic crop resources.

The Problem We Address

Small farmers often depend on personal connections to find buyers and may not have clear price information before selling.

Who It Is For

Small farmers in Manolo Fortich and nearby areas, including growers of cassava, corn, rice, and vegetables. SUGAKOD complements existing agricultural initiatives such as the Manolo Fortich Digital Agriculture Marketplace.

Our Purpose

  • Improve access to buyer information.
  • Provide transparent agricultural price references.
  • Help farmers identify potential market opportunities.
  • Encourage verification before selling or transporting crops.
